# Teaching

## How to talk and revise

Working with the planning skill, clarify learning needs through ongoing dialogue, and gradually form and adjust the teaching plan.

1. **Ask first, then form the content**: The AI asks the key questions of the current stage. After the learner answers, form the concrete content from the answers together with professional judgment. Where a clear answer already exists, carry that part forward as it is.
2. **Calibrate understanding by restating**: Restate or moderately expand what the learner means, for them to confirm, correct, and add to it, and gradually clarify the learning purpose, current level, preferences, and constraints.
3. **Calibrate needs through concrete results**: Provide a stage plan, sample materials, a knowledge inventory, or exercises, so the learner can discover differences through the concrete content, and then revise accordingly.
4. **Keep adjusting from actual learning**: Using the learner's feedback, exercise performance, and checkpoint tests, update the teaching plan; state what was adjusted and what the adjustment is based on.

The eight items below are what must be clarified and carried out step by step. Dialogue can move back and forth, and can return to earlier stages to revise them.

## Before learning begins

1. **Confirm the learning purpose**: Make clear what problem should be solved once learning is complete, what situations it will be used in, and what level should be reached.
2. **Judge the nature of the subject**: Analyze the knowledge structure and how ability is formed, and determine the place of understanding, memory, derivation, operation, and creation.
3. **Verify the current level**: Drawing on learning history, existing results, and diagnostic questions, confirm the abilities already mastered and the gaps in prerequisites.
4. **Design the teaching stages**: Divide into stages, and write out each stage's ability goals, checkpoint tests, and passing criteria.
5. **Collect and select teaching materials**: Around the stage goals, search for textbooks, courses, documents, cases, and exercises; check their sources, accuracy, difficulty, and completeness; and select materials suited to the current level. State each material's use, the content it corresponds to, and the order of use, and continue to add materials during teaching as actual needs require.

## After learning begins

6. **Write the knowledge inventory**: List the knowledge points systematically by stage. Explain the concepts, principles, prerequisite relationships, interconnections, conditions of use, and practical uses, and make clear how deeply each item needs to be mastered.
7. **Design integrated exercises**: Around the stage goals, combine multiple knowledge points into complete problems. Specify the input, task, constraints, expected output, and evaluation criteria, and use the exercises to test understanding and the ability to apply it.
8. **Feedback, acceptance, and adjustment**: Analyze the causes of errors, provide targeted practice for weak points, and adjust subsequent teaching based on the results of checkpoint tests.

When organizing the knowledge inventory, explanations, and exercises, organize the teaching from mature materials when they exist. When materials are insufficient, add explanations, original examples, and exercises, and mark the sources and the parts that were written originally.

# 教学

## 交流与修订方式

结合规划 Skill，通过持续交流明确学习需求，逐步形成和调整教学安排。

1. **先提问、再形成内容**：AI 围绕当前环节的关键问题提问，学习者回答后，结合回答与专业判断形成具体内容。已有明确答案的部分直接沿用。
2. **通过复述校准理解**：复述或适度展开学习者的意思，供其确认、纠正和补充，逐步明确学习目的、现有水平、偏好与约束。
3. **通过具体结果校准需求**：给出阶段方案、材料样例、知识清单或练习，让学习者通过具体内容发现差异，再据此修改。
4. **依据实际学习持续调整**：结合学习者反馈、练习表现与节点测试，更新教学安排；说明调整了什么、依据是什么。

以下八项是需要逐步明确和执行的内容，交流可以来回推进，也可以返回前面的环节修订。

## 开始学习前

1. **确认学习目的**：明确学完要解决什么问题、用于什么场景、达到什么程度。
2. **判断学科性质**：分析知识结构与能力形成方式，确定理解、记忆、推导、操作、创作各占什么位置。
3. **核实现有水平**：结合学习经历、已有成果和诊断题，确认已掌握的能力与前置缺口。
4. **设计教学阶段**：划分阶段，写清每阶段的能力目标、节点测试和通过标准。
5. **收集与筛选教学材料**：围绕阶段目标，检索教材、课程、文档、案例与练习，核查来源、准确性、难度和完整性，选出适合当前水平的材料；说明各份材料的用途、对应内容与使用顺序，并在教学中根据实际需要持续补充。

## 开始学习后

6. **书写知识清单**：按阶段系统列出知识点，说明概念、原理、前置关系、相互联系、适用条件与实际用途，明确各项需要掌握的深度。
7. **设计综合练习**：围绕阶段目标，把多个知识点结合成完整问题，明确输入、任务、约束、预期输出和评价标准，用练习检验理解与应用能力。
8. **反馈、验收与调整**：分析错误原因，针对薄弱环节补练，根据节点测试结果调整后续教学。

组织知识清单、讲解和练习时，有成熟材料则据此组织教学；材料不足时，补充讲解、自编示例和练习，并标明来源与自编部分。
