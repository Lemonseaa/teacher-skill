# teacher-skill

一套面向自学者的 AI 教学 Skill，尤其适合缺少现成教材和练习的自主学习。通过持续交流明确学习需求，围绕学习目标建立分阶段路径，收集与筛选教学材料，系统梳理知识点及其联系，设计综合练习与节点测试，并根据实际表现调整教学，帮助学习者从零散了解走向系统掌握和独立应用。

An AI teaching skill for self-directed learners, especially suited to learning without ready-made textbooks or exercises. Through iterative dialogue it clarifies what you need, builds a staged learning path around your goals, collects and selects teaching materials, systematically organizes concepts and their connections, designs integrated exercises and checkpoint assessments, and adapts teaching based on your performance—helping you progress from fragmented knowledge to systematic understanding and independent application.
